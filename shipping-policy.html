Shipping Policy

Effective Date: [Insert Date]

PawPawPetz is primarily a platform that connects pet parents with independent pet service providers. In limited cases, physical products such as pet accessories, merchandise, welcome kits, or partner items may be shipped either by PawPawPetz or third-party sellers. This Shipping Policy applies only to such physical shipments.

1. Processing Time
  • Orders are typically processed within 1–3 business days after successful payment confirmation.
  • Processing time may vary based on product availability, seller location, or operational delays.
  • Orders placed on weekends or public holidays will be processed on the next business day.
2. Shipping Options & Timeframes

Domestic (India): Estimated delivery time is 3–7 business days after dispatch.

International Orders (if applicable): Estimated delivery time is 7–15 business days, subject to customs clearance and destination country regulations.

Delivery timelines are estimates and not guaranteed.

3. Shipping Fees
  • Shipping charges, if applicable, will be clearly displayed at checkout.
  • Fees may vary based on:
    • Delivery location
    • Product size and weight
    • Shipping method selected
  • Promotional offers or free shipping may be available for select orders.
4. Order Tracking
  • Once an order is shipped, tracking details will be shared via email, SMS, or within the PawPawPetz app.
  • Tracking updates depend on the courier partner and may take up to 24 hours to reflect.
5. Customs, Duties, and Taxes (International Orders)
  • International shipments may be subject to customs duties, import taxes, or other charges imposed by the destination country.
  • These charges are not included in the product price or shipping fee and must be paid by the customer.
  • PawPawPetz is not responsible for delays caused by customs clearance.
6. Undeliverable or Returned Packages

An order may be returned or marked undeliverable due to:

  • Incorrect or incomplete shipping address
  • Multiple failed delivery attempts
  • Refusal to accept delivery

In such cases:

  • Reshipping charges may apply
  • Refunds, if any, will exclude shipping fees
  • Returned items must be unused and in original condition
7. Damaged or Lost Shipments
  • If your order arrives damaged, please notify us within 48 hours of delivery with supporting photos.
  • For lost shipments, investigations will be initiated with the courier partner.
  • Replacements or refunds will be processed only after verification and courier confirmation.
8. Service Disclaimer
  • PawPawPetz does not handle shipping for pet services such as grooming, walking, or boarding.
  • Any physical items exchanged directly between pet parents and service providers fall outside PawPawPetz’s responsibility.
